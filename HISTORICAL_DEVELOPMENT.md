# Historical development and provenance

## Source projects

- [RFT-SIRM/Evgeny-Theorem](https://github.com/RFT-SIRM/Evgeny-Theorem) — SU(2)-connected Sierpiński-gasket operators and the fourth-moment trace-defect theorem; higher moments remain a research program.
- [RFT-SIRM/Phi-Genesis](https://github.com/RFT-SIRM/Phi-Genesis) — signed Z₂-flux spectral-decimation, inertia, boundary/corner corrections, and exploratory numerical hypotheses.

## Provenance policy

This file is a verified chronology, not a retrospective narrative assembled from memory. Each dated milestone should include a source commit, tag, archived artifact, or reproducible test output. Where that evidence is missing, mark the date or claim as unverified.

## Initial synthesis

The new repository is intended to connect and audit the two research strands without conflating their operator definitions. It begins as a synthesis and evidence-management layer. It does not claim that every historical computation has been independently rerun.

## Required record for each milestone

- date and source repository;
- commit or release tag;
- exact change or mathematical claim;
- tests or formal proof run;
- outcome, including failures;
- remaining limitations.

Do not infer a historical cause or a successful proof from a commit message alone. Use source diffs, artifacts, and test records wherever possible.
