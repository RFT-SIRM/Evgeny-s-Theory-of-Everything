# Lean bridge plan for higher-moment gluing

**Purpose:** translate the paper-level trace-composition identity into the existing formalization architecture without changing the Lean repository prematurely.

## Existing formalized infrastructure

The current [GlueAlg.lean](https://github.com/RFT-SIRM/Evgeny-Theorem/blob/main/formalization/EvgenyTheorem/GlueAlg.lean) already provides:

- `GlueData`, the pairing matrix `G.N`, and the copy-block predicate `BD`;
- preservation of block diagonality under multiplication (`BD_mul`);
- the endpoint actions `N_mul` and `mul_N`;
- the corner collapse `Q_eq_diag` for `N * K * N);
- trace vanishing for a block-diagonal factor followed by `N` (`trace_BD_N`);
- specialized mixed-word lemmas `trace_mul_Q`, `trace_XNXN`, and `trace_KQKQ`;
- the fourth-power theorem `glue_trace4`, which currently relies on `trace_sub_pow4` in `TraceExpand.lean`.

This is a useful foundation: the formalization already proves several of the structural reductions needed for the fourth moment. It does not yet supply a general $p=6,8,10$ word-classification theorem.

## Recommended Lean decomposition

### A. Prove a generic trace-composition theorem

In `TraceExpand.lean`, introduce a theorem over square matrices stating

$$
\operatorname{Tr}((K+KN)^p)
=
\operatorname{Tr}(K^p)+
\sum_{r=1}^{p}\frac{p}{r}
\sum_{a_1+\cdots+a_r=p,\ a_i\ge1}
\operatorname{Tr}(K^{a_1}N\cdots K^{a_r}N).
$$

For Lean, avoid division-heavy bookkeeping if possible: first prove the denominator-free identity after multiplying through by $p!$ or use a finite-sum formulation whose coefficients are integer counts, then derive the rational-coefficient version over $\mathbb C$. The cyclic-word double count is documented in [TRACE_COMPOSITION_IDENTITY_PROOF.md](TRACE_COMPOSITION_IDENTITY_PROOF.md).

The theorem must use only distributivity and cyclicity of trace; it must not assume $KN=NK$.

### B. Separate word reduction from graph-specific hypotheses

Add lemmas in `GlueAlg.lean` for the generic mixed words generated by the composition theorem. Reuse `BD_mul`, `trace_BD_N`, and `Q_eq_diag` whenever their hypotheses apply. Avoid a monolithic expanded proof for each power.

For each composition $(a_1,\ldots,a_r)$, record whether its trace:
1. vanishes by block support;
2. collapses through $N K^j N$ to a diagonal corner expression;
3. reduces to products of diagonal entries of powers $K^j$ at paired corners; or
4. requires a separate off-diagonal corner term.

This classification is the key missing bridge between the abstract identity and the desired moment recurrence.

### C. Keep the degree-10 exceptional transition explicit

The independent finite-level calculation finds nonzero cross-corner blocks at powers 8 and 9 on level 3. Therefore, the first structural lemma should not assert a blanket vanishing statement for all mixed words. In particular, the transition $3\to4$ for degree 10 must retain the exceptional terms until they are computed or shown to cancel exactly.

### D. Formalize in stages

1. Prove and compile the general trace-composition identity alone.
2. Prove a reusable lemma reducing each mixed-word class under `GlueData` assumptions.
3. Derive the degree-6 identity first, since it has the smallest number of compositions among the unresolved higher moments.
4. Derive degree 8, then degree 10 with the exceptional terms explicit.
5. Only after the structural recurrence is proved, combine it with a finite exact base certificate to establish the all-level result.

## Verification boundary

This is a plan based on source inspection of the current `GlueAlg.lean` and `TraceExpand.lean`. No Lean file was changed and no Lean build was run as part of this update. Do not label any higher-moment theorem formalized until a separate Lean change compiles cleanly.


## E. Add a radius-to-moment lemma before expanding mixed words

The new [CORNER_LOCALITY_LEMMA.md](CORNER_LOCALITY_LEMMA.md) proves that if rooted radius-$R$ neighbourhoods preserve all directed operator blocks, then the diagonal root blocks of powers agree through degree $2R+1$. The proof expands $(H^j)_{oo}$ over closed walks and uses $d(o,v_t)\le\min(t,j-t)\le\lfloor j/2\rfloor$.

For radius $R=4$, this gives corner-block equality for powers $j\le9$, exactly the diagonal range used in the current moment analysis. The Lean development should formalize the finite-matrix closed-walk lemma independently of the graph construction first; then the graph-specific proof only needs to supply the rooted labelled isomorphism and block preservation.

This is a stronger and more modular bridge than separately proving level equality for each power. It does not eliminate the exceptional off-diagonal corner terms at powers 8 and 9 in the degree-10 transition.


## F. Prove geometric locality by self-similar induction

The new [CORNER_NEIGHBOURHOOD_INDUCTION.md](CORNER_NEIGHBOURHOOD_INDUCTION.md) gives a recursive proof for the rooted radius-4 graph ball with degree labels and face-index axis classes: for $m\ge3$, the nearest copy junction is at distance $2^m\ge8>4$, so the ball lies wholly inside one corner copy; face-list offsets are multiples of three and preserve the axis index modulo three.

This closes the purely geometric/axis-labelled locality statement on paper. It deliberately does **not** close the global vertex-index orientation/sign issue used by the current sparse operator. That remaining gap must be handled either by proving sign preservation for a recursive map or by a gauge-covariant block formulation. The finite strict directed-label checks through level 10 are useful evidence, not a substitute for this last all-level proof.
